# Guion de video · 2 minutos 30 segundos

## 0:00–0:18 · El problema

**Imagen:** Dashboard con la lista priorizada.

**Voz:**

> Schools often detect academic risk only after a learner has already failed, accumulated absences, or disengaged. AYNI Twin helps teachers act earlier, without labeling a student or pretending to predict their future.

## 0:18–0:35 · La propuesta

**Imagen:** Seleccionar a Ana Torres y abrir Student Twin.

**Voz:**

> AYNI Twin turns attendance, grade trends, and pending assignments into an explainable academic twin. The risk score is deterministic, every factor is visible, and all demo records are synthetic.

## 0:35–0:58 · Comprender

**Imagen:** Recorrer Resumen, Asignaturas y Registro docente.

**Voz:**

> Instead of a generic alert, the teacher sees what changed, when it changed, and where support may be most useful. Subject-level trends and teacher observations remain separated to avoid visual overload.

## 0:58–1:28 · Simular

**Imagen:** Future Lab. Cambiar asistencia, tutorías y actividades. Mostrar las tres curvas.

**Voz:**

> In Future Lab, the teacher can adjust five interventions and compare three possible trajectories. These are configurable assumptions for decision support, not guaranteed outcomes.

## 1:28–1:53 · Convertir en acción

**Imagen:** Crear el plan, mostrar la transición de análisis y revisar las acciones.

**Voz:**

> Amazon Bedrock produces a structured fourteen-day draft. If the model is unavailable, a validated fallback keeps the workflow running. The teacher can edit every action, responsible person, deadline, and success indicator before approval.

## 1:53–2:12 · Medir

**Imagen:** Aprobar y abrir Impact Proof.

**Voz:**

> Impact Proof compares the approved estimate with the observed result. The difference is not hidden: it becomes evidence for the next human decision.

## 2:12–2:30 · AWS y cierre

**Imagen:** Diagrama de arquitectura y regreso al dashboard.

**Voz:**

> AYNI Twin runs on AWS with Amplify, API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB, Bedrock, and CloudWatch. Built with an AI coding agent and designed for responsible, explainable, human-led support. Don’t just predict dropout. Simulate the intervention that can prevent it.
